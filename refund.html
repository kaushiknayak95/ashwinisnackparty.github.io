Refund Policy

We want every order to reach you in good condition. This policy explains when a refund may be considered.

1. When a refund may be considered

A refund may be considered when there is a significant issue with an order, such as receiving the wrong product or receiving a product that was damaged during delivery.

Each situation will be reviewed based on the details provided by the customer.

2. Food products and refunds

Our products are food items intended for consumption. For hygiene and food-safety reasons, we generally do not accept returns of correctly delivered food products simply because a customer changes their mind.

Refunds are therefore not normally available for correctly delivered products that are unwanted or no longer required.

3. Damaged products

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

Please provide your order details and clear photographs of the outer package and affected product where possible.

After reviewing the issue, we may offer an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or refund.

4. Incorrect products

If you receive a product that is different from what you ordered, please contact us promptly.

We will review the order and, where the error is confirmed, determine the appropriate resolution.

5. Order cancellation

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible after placing it.

If the order has not yet been processed or dispatched, cancellation may be possible.

Once an order has been processed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

6. Refund processing

If a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the applicable payment method or payment service used for the original transaction.

The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or bank.

7. Information required for a refund request

To help us investigate an issue, please provide:

  • Order details or payment information.
  • Your name and contact number.
  • A description of the issue.
  • Photographs of damaged or incorrect products where applicable.

8. Refund decisions

Refund requests are reviewed individually based on the circumstances of the order.

We reserve the right to request additional information when necessary to verify an issue before approving a refund.

9. Changes to this Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Need help with an order?

If you believe you are eligible for a refund or have received a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us.

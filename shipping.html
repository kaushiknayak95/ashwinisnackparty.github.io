Shipping & Returns

We want your snacks to reach you fresh, safely packed and on time. Please read our shipping and return guidelines before placing your order.

🚚 Delivery estimate

Orders are generally expected to be delivered within 2–5 days after successful payment.

Delivery times may vary depending on the delivery location, courier service and circumstances outside our control.

Shipping charges

Shipping charges are calculated based on the order value and total shipment weight.

  • ₹800 or more: Free shipping.
  • Up to 1 kg: ₹100 shipping.
  • Above 1 kg up to 2 kg: ₹150 shipping.
  • Above 2 kg up to 3 kg: ₹200 shipping.
  • Above 3 kg: Shipping starts at ₹250, with additional charges applicable for weight above 5 kg.

The applicable shipping charge will be shown during checkout before payment is completed.

Order processing

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation.

Please make sure that your delivery address, phone number and other contact details are entered correctly during checkout.

Incorrect or incomplete delivery information may cause delays or unsuccessful delivery.

Food product returns

Because our products are food items and are packed for consumption, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges for products that have been delivered correctly.

We recommend checking your order as soon as it is delivered.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, contains the wrong product, or has another significant issue, please contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

Please provide your order details and, where applicable, clear photographs of the package and product so that we can review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

Delivery delays

While we aim to deliver orders within the estimated timeframe, delays can occasionally occur because of courier operations, weather, holidays, transport disruptions or other circumstances beyond our control.

If your order is significantly delayed, please contact us and we will assist you in checking its status.

Cancellation

If you need to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible after placing the order.

Once an order has been processed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

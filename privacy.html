Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling the information you provide to us responsibly.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order through our website, we may collect information needed to process and deliver your order.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Area, city, state and PIN code
  • Order and payment-related information

2. How we use your information

We use the information provided by you to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders.
  • Arrange delivery of your products.
  • Contact you regarding your order when necessary.
  • Respond to customer enquiries and support requests.
  • Maintain records relating to orders and transactions.

3. Payment information

Online payments are processed through our payment service provider.

We do not intend to store your complete debit card, credit card or banking credentials on our website.

Payment information is handled through the payment provider's secure payment infrastructure.

4. Cookies and local storage

Our website may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to remember information such as your shopping cart and checkout details during your visit.

These technologies help the website provide a smoother shopping experience.

5. Sharing information

We may share necessary order information with service providers involved in fulfilling your order, such as payment processors and delivery or courier services.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Order communications

We may use the contact information you provide to communicate with you about your order, payment, delivery or customer support request.

These communications are necessary for fulfilling and supporting your order.

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with your orders and customer interactions.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-party services

Our website may rely on third-party services for functions such as online payments, website hosting and other technical services.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Your choices

You may contact us if you have questions about information associated with an order or if you would like to request clarification regarding how your information is handled.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page.
